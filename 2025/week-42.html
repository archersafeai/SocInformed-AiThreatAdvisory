Week 42: Oct 13, 2025 – Oct 19, 2025

2 real, sourced AI-security events identified this week — 1 Critical, 1 High.

ServiceNow Now Assist second-order prompt injection patched

High
patches shipped October 2025

AppOmni and NeuralTrust disclosed that default configurations in ServiceNow's Now Assist agent platform allowed low-privileged data to trigger second-order prompt injection — letting one AI agent enlist another to bypass access controls, escalate privileges, or exfiltrate CRM and ticket data. ServiceNow shipped patches to hosted instances in October 2025.

Source: AppOmni / NeuralTrust, Oct 2025 — https://karanbansal.in/ai-security-timeline/

CamoLeak (GitHub Copilot Chat exfiltration) publicly disclosed

Critical
October 2025

The CamoLeak vulnerability found by Legit Security in June 2025 and mitigated by GitHub in August was formally publicly disclosed with full technical detail in October 2025, drawing broader industry attention to Camo-proxy-based exfiltration as an attack class against AI coding assistants.
