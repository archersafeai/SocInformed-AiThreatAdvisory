Wiz Research finds DeepSeek's internal database exposed to the internet

Critical
Jan 29, 2025

Wiz Research found a publicly accessible, unauthenticated ClickHouse database belonging to DeepSeek within minutes of starting to look. The database — hosted at oauth2callback.deepseek.com:9000 and dev.deepseek.com:9000 — exposed over a million log-stream records including plaintext chat history, API keys, and backend operational metadata, with logs dating back to Jan 6, 2025. DeepSeek secured the exposure promptly after Wiz's responsible disclosure.

Source: Wiz Research, Jan 29, 2025 — https://www.wiz.io/blog/wiz-research-uncovers-exposed-deepseek-database-leak
